What It Is

Biz Analyst is a mobile and desktop app that connects to your Tally and puts your business data on your phone. It syncs securely with your TallyPrime, so you can view sales, outstanding payments, stock and reports in real time — without sitting at the office computer. Kanvtech sets it up for you and provides support.

Who It's For

Business owners, accountants and sales teams who use Tally and need their numbers on the go. Ideal if you travel, run multiple locations, or want to check your business without depending on your accounts team.

Key Features

Biz Analyst brings everything you track in Tally to your phone, so you always know where your business stands.

Real-time access to your Tally data on Android and iOS

View sales, collections, outstanding, stock, expenses and ledgers

Create and share invoices, orders, receipts and statements via WhatsApp or email

Automated payment reminders to customers to improve cash flow

Dashboards and graphs to spot sales trends

Works offline with data from your last sync

Role-based access so staff see only what they should

Add multiple companies from your Tally
