Biometric Attendance Systems for Your Business

Kanvtech supplies, installs and supports biometric attendance systems that replace manual registers and stop proxy punching. This includes Bio360, our own smart biometric range, alongside trusted brands like ESSL, Biomax and ZKTeco. Every device connects to your HRMS and flows straight into attendance, leave and payroll.

What It Is

From Paper Registers to Accurate Attendance

A biometric attendance system records exactly when your staff arrive and leave, using fingerprint, face, card, PIN or QR. Kanvtech provides the devices, connects them to your HRMS software, and manages the full setup. Attendance is captured in real time and feeds directly into payroll. We offer our own Bio360 devices as well as leading third-party brands, so you get the right fit for your business.

Manual attendance registers are slow to fill, easy to manipulate, and hard to audit. Kanvtech replaces them with a digital system that captures every punch the moment it happens, keeping accurate, tamper-resistant records your team can rely on.

Our Own Smart Biometric Brand

Bio360 — Connect. Sync. Manage.

Bio360 is Kanvtech's own range of smart biometric devices, built for accurate attendance across offices, branches and remote sites. It captures attendance through fingerprint, face, RFID card, PIN or QR, and syncs in real time with your systems. Accurate, secure, durable and built to scale with your business.
