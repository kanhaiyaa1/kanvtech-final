Biometric Attendance Systems for Your Business

Biometric devices capture accurate attendance the moment your team clocks in, removing manual registers and proxy punching. Paired with HRMS software, that data flows directly into attendance, leave and Spine Payroll records. Kanvtech supplies the devices, sets up the software, and keeps the whole system running for you.

What It Is

From Manual Registers to Accurate Attendance

Biometric attendance replaces manual registers with fingerprint and face-recognition devices that record exactly when staff arrive and leave. Kanvtech supplies the hardware, connects it to HRMS software, and manages the whole setup. Attendance flows straight into Spine Payroll, so hours and salaries stay accurate.

Why It Matters

From Paper Registers to Digital Records

Manual attendance registers are slow to fill, easy to manipulate, and hard to audit. Kanvtech replaces them with a digital system that captures every punch the moment it happens, keeping accurate, tamper-resistant records your team can rely on.

Key Features

Everything you need to track attendance accurately, from the device to the Spine Payroll register.

Biometric Punch Devices

Fingerprint and face-recognition devices from trusted brands including ESSL, Biomax and ZKTeco.

Remote Punch-In

Field and remote staff can punch in with a selfie and their location, from anywhere.

Real-Time Cloud Sync

HRMS & Spine Payroll Integration

Web & Mobile Management

Multi-Branch Attendance

How It Works

Every Punch, Instantly Confirmed

Each biometric device verifies identity in seconds and records the exact time of every punch. That data syncs to the cloud in real time, so attendance from every branch and location comes together in one place, ready for Spine Payroll.
