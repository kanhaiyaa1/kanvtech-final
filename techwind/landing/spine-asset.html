Why Spine

Purpose-Built for Fixed Asset Management

Spine Asset is a dedicated fixed asset management platform, built for accuracy, audit-readiness and long-term financial control. Kanvtech, an authorised Spine Gold Partner, configures the platform to match how your business tracks, maintains and reports on what it owns.

What It Is

From Scattered Spreadsheets to Clarity

Fixed assets such as machinery, IT equipment, vehicles and furniture represent some of the largest investments a business makes. Spine Asset replaces spreadsheets and paper registers with a centralised, automated platform: one place to record every asset, calculate depreciation automatically, and maintain an accurate, current asset register.

It takes asset management from chaos to clarity.

Who It's For

Any Business With Assets Worth Tracking

Spine Asset serves businesses across manufacturing, infrastructure, textiles, healthcare, automobile and agriculture. It suits companies managing equipment across multiple locations, organisations that require clean records at audit time, and teams responsible for warranties, AMCs and maintenance schedules.

Core Capabilities

What's Included

Asset Tracking & Register

A complete, centralised register of every asset, with location, custodian, department and status available anytime.

Depreciation Management

Automated depreciation across multiple methods and asset categories, with tax and accounting books kept accurate.

Barcode & QR Code Integration

Tag every asset with a barcode or QR code for instant identification and faster, error-free audits.

Physical Verification

Scan, confirm and reconcile actual asset status against records digitally, in a fraction of the usual time.

Approval Workflows & Audit Trails

Maker-checker controls ensure every acquisition, transfer or disposal is authorised and fully traceable.

Asset Allocation

Assign and track assets by employee, department, cost centre or project, with a complete movement history.

Warranty, AMC & Insurance Tracking

Automated alerts for warranties, AMCs, insurance renewals and scheduled maintenance keep every deadline covered.

Document Management

Attach invoices, purchase records, photographs and service documents directly to each asset.

Multi-Company & Multi-Location

Manage assets across multiple companies, branches and locations from a single, unified platform.
