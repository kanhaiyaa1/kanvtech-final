What It Is

From Scattered Spreadsheets to Clarity

Spine Asset is fixed asset management software that brings order to how your business tracks and maintains its assets. Instead of scattered spreadsheets and missed renewals, you get one place to record every asset, calculate depreciation automatically, and know exactly what you own.

It takes asset management from chaos to clarity.

Who It's For

Any Business With Assets Worth Tracking

Used across industries like manufacturing, infrastructure, textiles, healthcare, automobile and agriculture. It suits companies managing equipment across multiple locations, those needing clean records at audit time, and teams that keep losing track of warranties, AMCs and maintenance schedules.

Key Features

What's Included

  • Asset tracking across locations, with status and assignment
  • Automated depreciation calculation and scheduling
  • Fixed asset register with full purchase and status history
  • Barcode integration for quick, error-free asset tracking
  • Physical verification to match real assets against records
  • Warranty, AMC and insurance tracking with timely reminders
  • Access levels and audit trail (maker-checker) for control
  • Custom reports and accounting software integration
