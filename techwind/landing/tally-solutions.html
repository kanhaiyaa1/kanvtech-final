TallyPrime

One Platform for Accounting, GST & Inventory

TallyPrime brings accounting, inventory, banking and statutory compliance together in a single, integrated platform. It is built for business owners, accountants and new users alike, with a simple, consistent interface that does not require deep accounting knowledge to get started. TallyPrime runs on a single PC (Silver), across a network (Gold), or on the cloud.

Whether you are a first-time entrepreneur or an experienced accountant, TallyPrime fits naturally into how you already work, and grows with your business.

Key Capabilities

What's Included

Accounting & Invoicing

Create sales, purchase, journal, payment and receipt vouchers quickly, with daily, weekly, monthly and yearly reports available instantly.

Inventory Management

Manage unlimited stock groups, categories, multiple godowns, batches and price lists, with real-time stock visibility.

GST & Statutory Compliance

File GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B, generate e-invoices and e-way bills, directly from TallyPrime, without visiting the GST portal separately.

Connected Banking

Initiate payments, track their status and reconcile bank transactions directly inside TallyPrime.

Business Reports & Dashboards

Real-time, customisable reports and a graphical dashboard give instant insight into business performance.

Multi-Company Management

Handle multiple businesses under one licence, with consolidated group reporting.

Edit Log & Audit Trail

Complete transaction-level tracking strengthens process control and compliance.

SmartFind & WhatsApp

Locate any transaction instantly, and share invoices and reports directly by WhatsApp or email.

Multi-Currency Transactions

Built for businesses that transact with clients and suppliers abroad.

TallyPrime Server

Built for Growing, Multi-User Teams

TallyPrime Server is an enterprise-class upgrade over the Gold licence, built for businesses with 10 or more simultaneous users, or multiple branches reporting into a head office. It brings the speed, security and control a growing team needs, and works alongside your existing TallyPrime Gold licence.

High Concurrency

Multiple users load companies, save transactions, export reports and take backups at the same time, without downtime.

Centralised Data Access

A dedicated server architecture keeps company data secure and consistently accessible.

Session Monitoring

Real-time visibility into user sessions lets administrators track, manage or disconnect users as needed.

Enhanced Security

Centralised control over user access and activity logging strengthens data security.

Branch Consolidation

Multi-branch data consolidates at the head office while local operations continue without interruption.

Tally Software Services (TSS)

A Connected Layer on Top of Tally

TSS is Tally's own subscription that keeps TallyPrime current, bringing the latest technology, statutory updates and connected services together in one place. Every new TallyPrime licence includes TSS for the first year, and it needs renewal after that to keep these connected features active.

Free Product Upgrades

Every major release and enhancement is included at no extra cost, keeping TallyPrime current with statutory and technology changes as they happen.

Connected GST Services

File GST returns, generate e-invoices and e-way bills, and reconcile input tax credit, without visiting the GST portal separately.

Connected Banking & Payments

Initiate payments, track their status and reconcile bank transactions in real time, directly from TallyPrime, without switching to a banking app.

Automatic Bank Reconciliation

Regular updates to cheque formats and bank statement layouts keep reconciliation accurate, so your records always match the bank data.

Cloud Backup & Data Sync

Scheduled backups run automatically with free cloud storage included, and data stays synced between your head office and branches without manual effort.

Remote Access

Access company data securely from anywhere through Tally.NET, with your data kept safe and never stored on Tally's own servers.

Auto-Scan & Mobile Reports

Scan invoices to create transactions automatically, cutting manual entry, and check your key business reports from your phone, wherever you are.
