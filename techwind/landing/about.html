Introduction

Kanvtech Solutions Private Limited is a complete IT solutions company, backed by over 18 years of combined experience in business software and enterprise IT. Our team has worked across HRMS, payroll, accounting, cloud infrastructure and custom software development, building systems that businesses rely on every day. We work with organisations of every size, across manufacturing, retail, healthcare, services, finance and education.

As a Spine Gold Partner, we configure Spine HRMS, Payroll and Asset to Spine's own implementation standards, and we provide Tally software, setup and customisation entirely through our in-house team. Our approach starts with understanding how your business runs, and we continue to provide training, updates and troubleshooting as your requirements evolve.
