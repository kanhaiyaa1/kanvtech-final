What It Is & Why It Matters

Software Made for Your Exact Process

Custom software means solutions built specifically for your business instead of ready-made products you have to adapt to. When off-the-shelf tools don't fit, have too many unused features, or miss the one thing you actually need, we build software that matches your workflow perfectly.

The result: a precise fit with no workarounds, software that grows with your business without per-user fees, full ownership with no vendor lock-in, and lower long-term costs than recurring packaged licences.

Who It's For

When Standard Software Isn't Enough

Our Own Field Workforce Product

Live Employee Tracking with Our Module — Fieldzust

Fieldzust is Kanvtech's own field workforce management platform. It helps businesses track their on-ground teams in real time, assign and monitor tasks, manage attendance, and turn field activity into clear reports. Built for any business with staff working outside the office.

Track — People, places and work in real time.

Real-Time Field Visibility

Track your field teams live on an interactive map. Know exactly where your people are, how long they spend at each location, and their route history. All updates happen in real time, so you always have the complete picture of your on-ground operations.

Task & Attendance Management

Create and assign tasks directly to field teams with built-in checklists, photos, and notes. Track attendance, timesheets and task completion in one place. Every update syncs instantly, so your office staff always knows the latest status of field activities.
