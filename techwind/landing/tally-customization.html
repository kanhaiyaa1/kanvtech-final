What It Is

Shaped to Fit Your Business

Tally is powerful out of the box, but every business runs a little differently. Tally customization is about shaping it to fit yours: changing how information is captured, displayed and reported, and connecting it with the other tools you use.

The best part is that this happens without touching Tally's core, so you keep everything stable while getting exactly what you need.

Who It's For

When Standard Tally Doesn't Cover Everything

Typical needs we handle: customised reports the default version doesn't offer, integrating Tally with your CRM or other software, and capturing additional information in your Tally masters or vouchers. If you're exporting data and reworking it elsewhere, customization usually removes that step.

Key Features

What's Included

  • Custom reports built around the numbers and formats you use
  • Extra fields in masters and vouchers to capture the details you need
  • Integration between Tally and your CRM, software or digital tools
  • A unified flow of data across finance and operations
  • Works whether your Tally is on-site or on the cloud
  • Customization done without disturbing Tally's core functionality
