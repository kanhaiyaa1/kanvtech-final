Solutions for Every Industry

Our software and services adapt to how your industry works, whether that's a shop floor or a back office.

Manufacturing

From shift-based attendance to multi-branch payroll, Kanvtech keeps your factory floor running on time. Spine Payroll handles PF, ESIC and statutory compliance automatically, while Tally keeps production costs and inventory accounted for. Nothing slips through the cracks, even as you scale across plants.

Retail & Trading

Whether you run one store or twenty, Tally Prime keeps your billing, GST and stock in sync in real time. Move it to the cloud with us and check sales, stock and accounts from your phone. No more waiting on the evening report from each branch.

Healthcare

Hospitals and clinics run on people. Spine HR and Payroll manage shift rosters, attendance and salaries for doctors, nurses and support staff without the paperwork pile-up. Data stays secure, compliance is handled, and your team can focus on patients.

Services & Consulting

Billable hours, client expenses and project timesheets are hard to track in a spreadsheet. We build custom software shaped around how your consultancy actually bills and delivers work, so nothing gets missed and every client engagement stays profitable.

Education

Manage teaching and non-teaching staff payroll, HR records and student fee accounting in one system. Kanvtech sets up Spine and Tally together so your institute's people and finance side run smoothly across every academic term.

Finance & Accounting

Accounting and finance firms need accuracy first. We set up and support Tally Prime with the customisation, reporting and cloud access that keeps your books audit-ready, plus the compliance tools to keep client filings on schedule.
