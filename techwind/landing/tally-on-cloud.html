Run your TallyPrime from any device, any location, with secure 24x7 cloud access, set up and managed by Kanvtech.

What It Is

Your Existing Tally, Anywhere

Tally on Cloud lets you use your existing TallyPrime from anywhere instead of being tied to one office computer. Your Tally runs on a secure cloud server, and you log in to it from your laptop, home system or browser whenever you need.

Your team can work on the same data together, in real time, without anyone being in the same room.

Who It's For

Accounts Beyond the Office

Owners who travel, teams spread across locations or warehouses, accountants who work remotely, or growing companies adding more users and channels. You'll need an existing TallyPrime licence, which we can help arrange.

Key Features

What's Included

  • Secure 24x7 access to your Tally from any location or device
  • Multiple users working on the same data in real time
  • Dual-factor authentication and secure PIN login
  • Automatic, encrypted cloud backups with easy recovery
  • Self-service portal to manage users, systems and backups
  • Works across Windows, Linux and macOS (via browser)
  • Flexible plans by users and subscription period, no data limits
