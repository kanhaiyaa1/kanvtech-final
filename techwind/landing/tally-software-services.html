What It Is

A Connected Layer on Top of Tally

TSS is a subscription that adds a layer of connected services on top of your TallyPrime. It's what keeps your software current with the latest features and statutory changes, and unlocks online abilities like GST filing, connected banking, cloud backup and remote access to your reports.

In short, it turns Tally from a standalone tool into a connected one.

Who It's For

Any Business on TallyPrime

For anyone who wants more than offline accounting: handling GST and e-invoicing inside Tally, connected banking, backing up to the cloud, syncing data across branches, or checking reports from a phone. Every new TallyPrime licence comes with TSS for the first year; after that, it needs renewal.

What TSS Gives You

Everything Connected

  • Connected GST: download, reconcile invoices, file GSTR-1/3B, e-invoices & e-way bills
  • Connected banking & payments, with UPI/QR payment links
  • TallyDrive: automatic, secure cloud backup with easy recovery
  • Free product upgrades and major releases
  • Online access to your business reports from any device
  • Online data sync and consolidation across branches
  • WhatsApp for Business integration
