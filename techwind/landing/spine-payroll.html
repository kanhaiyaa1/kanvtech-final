What It Is

Payroll Without the Month-End Pressure

Spine Payroll is payroll software that takes the month-end pressure off your HRMS team. It handles salary calculations, payslips, deductions and statutory compliance automatically, with all your employee and payroll data in one place.

What used to take days of manual work and double-checking now runs accurately in a fraction of the time.

Who It's For

Any Business Running Monthly Payroll

From small teams to large companies across every industry. It's ideal if you're still calculating salaries on spreadsheets, struggling to keep up with PF, ESIC and tax rules, or simply want payroll that's quick, accurate and compliant without the stress.

Key Features

What's Included

  • High-speed, automated salary computation and disbursement
  • Statutory compliance built in: PF, ESIC, PT, TDS, Gratuity, Bonus and LWF
  • Customisable payslips with clear earnings and deductions
  • Loans and advances setup with repayment tracking
  • Backdated payments, arrears and adjustments handled smoothly
  • Complete employee data bank with up-to-date records
  • Custom report writer and dashboards for payroll insights
  • Role-based access and a secure maker-checker workflow
  • Mobile app (Android & iOS) for employees and admins
