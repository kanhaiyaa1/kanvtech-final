Spine Payroll

Payroll Without the Month-End Pressure

Spine Payroll runs salary calculations, payslips, deductions and statutory compliance automatically, with every employee and payroll record in one place. It suits any business running monthly payroll, from small teams to large companies still managing salaries on spreadsheets.

  • Statutory compliance built in: PF, ESIC, PT, TDS, Gratuity, Bonus and LWF, with tax tables that update automatically
  • Automated salary computation, disbursement and customisable payslips
  • Direct bank integration for accurate, on-time payments
  • Loans, advances, arrears and backdated payments handled cleanly
  • Custom report writer, role-based access and a mobile app

Spine HRMS

One Platform, Hire to Retire

Spine HRMS brings the full employee journey into one connected system: recruitment, attendance, payroll, leaves and performance, right through to exit. It replaces scattered spreadsheets and suits any business, from a small startup to a large enterprise, that is finding HR harder to manage by hand. The suite is modular, so you start with what you need and add modules as you grow.
